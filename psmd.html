Prettysweet Mostlydope was created by scarmosttdope in 2016.

“The name came to me during a period of true self-discovery in my early college years, when thinking about what makes me… me.”

prettysweet

The representation of feminine energy.

mostlydope

The “extra’d out”, limitless version of myself.

rough + tough + off the wall.

But the P.S.M.D world isn’t only for women. It’s an attitude & mindset. Prettysweet can be interpreted for what it is.

The vision for the brand is to create a place where creative + athletic + expressive people feel seen in their daily lives, but also while doing what they love.

Prettysweet Mostlydope will tap into

  • Fashion
  • Action Sports
  • Tech
  • Music
  • Entertainment

There is so much more to this than what it is right now. Your support along the journey is already greatly appreciated.
