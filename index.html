About SCAR

Graphic designer based in Los Angeles, working with South Bay Creative and Working Girl Collective.

Wearing your SCAR is an outward symbol of self-awareness, & self-acceptance. A statement that says, I am more than any obstacle life has thrown at me, without saying it.

I am a human being human ... because I SCAR.
