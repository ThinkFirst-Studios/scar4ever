This is the honest audit. It is not a bad site. It has real problems.

1. **The portfolio pages are image-only.** `/graphics`, `/logodesigns` and `/merch-design` return essentially zero text — no captions, no client names, no alt text. Every project name listed above lives on the *index* page; the actual work pages are bare galleries. Google cannot read any of it, and neither can a screen reader. Her best proof of work is invisible to search.
2. **The homepage leads with photography, not design.** The H1s are *"book your next photoshoot with me"* and *"shot by scar."* Her Instagram bio says **Graphic Designer**. The site's first impression argues with her own positioning.
3. **Heavy pages.** Homepage HTML alone is ~210KB before images; the services page ~269KB. Squarespace template weight on a mobile-first audience arriving from Instagram.
4. **The identity is generic-Squarespace.** Archivo Black on a stock template. Nothing on the site looks like the work *in* the portfolio — which is loud, hand-made and unmistakable. **This is the real pitch.** Her site undersells her by looking calmer than she is.
5. **Three markets, no local pages.** Honolulu, El Paso and Los Angeles appear as one line of text on the contact page. No per-market page, no local search presence in any of the three.
6. **No email address anywhere.** Form only. Brands that want to send a brief have to fill in a form and wait 24 hours.

## Art Direction

Her work is loud, hand-made, street-rooted. Match her — do not tame her. The failure mode on this build is making it tasteful.

**Ground:** true black `#000000`. Everything sits on black.

**Palette**

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Hot magenta / shocking pink** | Full saturation, in quantity. Big type, fills, glows. The dominant colour. |
| Secondary | **Acid yellow-green** | Accents, ticker, hover states |
| Secondary | **Pure red** | Sparingly — inside the pink, as in her flame piece |
| Neutral | **Chrome / silver, white** | Type accents, rules, chrome-effect edges |

Sample the exact hex values off the supplied artwork rather than guessing. The pink is the brand — don't mute it, don't pastel it, don't gradient it into softness.

**Type**
- **Display:** heavy condensed or wide sans, set enormous and tight. Headlines run edge to edge and sometimes crop off the canvas.
- **Wordmark:** lowercase **"scar"** in a soft rounded heavy face — her signature treatment.
- **Body:** one plain workhorse sans at readable sizes.
- She currently runs **Archivo Black** + **Space Grotesk**. Either is a legitimate starting point — Archivo Black in particular is already hers.

**Texture** — build with CSS, don't draw artwork:
halftone dot screens · spray-paint grain overlay · print misregistration (offset channel duplicates) · layered collage edges · marquee/ticker strips. `mix-blend-mode`, repeating background patterns, a subtle grain layer.

**Motion** — restrained but present:
horizontal scrolling ticker · hover states that shift and offset type · images scale slightly on hover. Scroll reveals with stagger. **No parallax soup.** Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Gradients:** used as *glow* and as *photo scrim*, not as decoration. Magenta→red inside the flame register, magenta→black for image overlays. Two to three stops, same family. Never a rainbow, never a mesh.

⛔ **Avoid:** gradient-mesh backgrounds, glassmorphism, rounded-card grids, corporate whitespace, stock iconography, emoji.
